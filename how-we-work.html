How we work

See the work before you commit to anything

Every engagement starts the same way, whichever service you choose: a short call, two weeks of real work for free, and a written quote only if you want to continue.

  1. Day 0 · 20 minutes

    Intro call

    How you work today, your tools, what you want fixed first and any customers or suppliers that need special care.

  2. Days 1–3

    Setup

    Access agreed at the lowest level that works, templates written and approved by you, data agreement signed.

  3. Days 1–14 · free

    Trial on real work

    We do the work and send a short update every working day.

  4. Day 14

    Review and quote

    A one-page summary of results and a fixed monthly quote for your volume.

Pricing

A fixed monthly fee, quoted for your volume

We do not publish a price list because the work depends on your volume: how many invoices or bills you have, or how much ad spend we manage. After the trial you get a fixed monthly fee in writing.

Engagements run month to month with 30 days' notice. There are no setup fees for the trial.
