Frequently asked questions

How does the free 2-week trial work?
After a first consultation, we set up and manage your campaigns for 14 days at zero cost. No credit card, no commitment. If you see results and want to continue, we move to a monthly plan.
Is the ad spend included in the price?
No. Our monthly fee is a management fee only. You pay the ad budget directly to the platform (Meta, Google, etc.). This keeps things transparent — you always know exactly where your ad money goes.
Are there any contracts?
None. Month-to-month only. Cancel any time with 30 days notice. We believe clients should stay because of results, not paperwork.
Can I upgrade or downgrade plans?
Yes, anytime. Most clients start on Starter or Growth and upgrade once they see ROI. Downgrading is also fine — just let us know before the next billing cycle.
