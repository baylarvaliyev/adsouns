The routine

A standard follow-up sequence, adjusted to you

You approve the wording once. Customers you mark as sensitive are handled by hand or not contacted at all.

WhenWhat happensTone
3 days before dueCourtesy reminder with the invoice attachedFriendly

What we need from you

One export, one mailbox, one call

  • An aged receivables report from your accounting software, or read-only access to it.
  • A mailbox on your domain, for example accounts@yourcompany.com, that we send from.
  • A 20-minute call to agree tone, sensitive customers and open disputes.

What we never do

Clear limits

  • We never collect money into our own account. Customers pay you, as they do today.
  • We never threaten, and any legal step is your decision.
  • We only chase business customers, never individual consumers.
  • We never chase an invoice before checking it has not already been paid.
