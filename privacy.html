Privacy notice

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Who we are

adsonus is a finance operations and marketing services business based in Baku, Azerbaijan. For questions about this notice or your data, contact baylar@adsonus.com.

What we collect on this website

  • Quote requests: the details you enter in our form (name, work email, company, country, website, phone and your message). The form is delivered to us by Web3Forms, which processes the submission on our behalf.
  • Usage data: we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how the website is used, such as pages visited and the device type. These tools may set cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote, because you asked us to (steps before entering a contract).
  • To improve the website and measure our marketing, based on our legitimate interest in running the business.

How long we keep it

Quote requests that do not lead to work are deleted within 24 months. Data about clients is kept for the length of the engagement and as required by law and our contract with you.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as email, form and analytics providers. Some of them are located outside the UK and European Union; where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Client data

When we work for a client, we handle their customers' and suppliers' data as a processor under a separate data processing agreement. That agreement, not this notice, governs that data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise these rights, email baylar@adsonus.com.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the date of the latest version at the top.
