Our rules

What we will and won't do with your business

You give us access to your customers, your suppliers and your numbers. These rules apply to every client and every service.

01

Your money never passes through us

Customers pay you directly. Payments to suppliers leave from your bank and are released by you.

02

You approve before anything goes out

Email templates, payment runs and ad budgets are signed off by you before they are used.

03

The least access we need

Read-only or limited roles wherever your software allows. Passwords are never sent by email or chat.

04

A data agreement from day one

We sign a data processing agreement covering what we hold, why, for how long and how it is deleted.

05

Your name, your tone

We write as part of your team and in your style. No threats, and any legal step stays your decision.

06

No lock-in

Monthly engagement with 30 days' notice. Everything we produce for you is yours to keep.

Questions

Before you ask

Anything else, write to info@adsonus.com and you will get an answer the same working day.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is quoted for your volume: how many invoices or bills, or how much ad spend we manage. After a short call you get a fixed monthly fee in writing. The 2-week trial is free.

Who is this for?

Small and medium businesses that sell to other businesses on invoice, typically 5 to 200 people, without a full finance team or an in-house marketing specialist.

Do you ever handle our money?

No. Your customers pay you directly. For accounts payable we prepare the payment run and you release it from your own bank.

What access do you need?

For receivables: a mailbox on your domain (for example accounts@yourcompany.com) and read-only access to your accounting software. For payables: a limited user role. For advertising: access to your ad accounts, which stay in your name.

Where are you based?

In Baku, Azerbaijan. We work UK and European business hours and reply to messages the same working day.

Can we use only one service?

Yes. Most clients start with one. The services work independently and share the same weekly reporting format.

Tell us what you need

Describe your situation in a few lines. You get a reply within one working day and a written quote after a short call.
