Before you ask

Anything else, write to baylar@adsonus.com and you will get an answer the same working day.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is quoted for your volume: how many invoices or bills, or how much ad spend we manage. After a short call you get a fixed monthly fee in writing. The 2-week trial is free.

Who is this for?

Small and medium businesses that sell to other businesses on invoice, typically 5 to 200 people, without a full finance team or an in-house marketing specialist.

Why not just turn on reminders in QuickBooks or Xero?

Automatic reminders are a good start, and we use them where they help. But software only sends emails. It does not call the person who approves payments, find out why an invoice is stuck, resend a missing purchase order or tell you which customers are getting slower. That is the part we do.

Do you ever handle our money?

No. Your customers pay you directly. For accounts payable we prepare the payment run and you release it from your own bank.

What access do you need?

For receivables: a mailbox on your domain (for example accounts@yourcompany.com) and read-only access to your accounting software. For payables: a limited user role. For advertising: access to your ad accounts, which stay in your name.

Where are you based?

In Baku, Azerbaijan. Our working day overlaps with US Eastern business hours, and we reply to messages the same business day.

Can we use only one service?

Yes. Many businesses start with one. The services work independently and share the same weekly reporting format.
