Before you ask

Anything else, write to contact@adsonus.com and you will get an answer the same working day.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is quoted for your volume: invoices or bills, leads, customer conversations, open roles or the size of your team. You get a fixed monthly fee in writing before anything starts. Month to month, with 30 days' notice.

Who is this for?

Small and medium businesses, typically 5 to 200 people, that need back-office work done properly but do not want to hire a full team for each function.

Why not hire someone in-house?

For many small businesses one function is not a full-time job, and one hire covers one skill. We cover several functions with documented routines, for a fixed monthly fee, and you can change the scope as you grow.

Do you ever handle our money?

No. Your customers pay you directly. For accounts payable we prepare the payment run and you release it from your own bank.

What access do you need?

The lowest access that does the job: a mailbox on your domain, limited roles in your accounting, CRM, help-desk or HR software, and delegated admin for IT support. Everything stays in your name.

Where are you based, and what hours do you work?

We are at Rashid Behbudov 66, Baku, Azerbaijan, and work with clients around the world. Working hours and response times are agreed in writing for your time zone.

Can we use only one service?

Yes. Many businesses start with one. Every service works on its own and shares the same weekly reporting format.
