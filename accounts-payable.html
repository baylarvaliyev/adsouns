Supplier bills paid on time, never twice

Bills arrive in one inbox. We capture, code and check them, then prepare a payment run that you approve and release from your own bank.

From inbox to approved payment run

  • One inbox for every billSuppliers send to a dedicated address on your domain. Paper bills can be photographed and forwarded.
  • Captured and codedSupplier, amount, dates and account code entered in your accounting software, with the bill attached.
  • Checked before anything is dueMatched against purchase orders or delivery notes where you use them, and checked for duplicates.
  • Payment run preparedA list of what to pay this week, ready for you to approve. You release it from your bank.
  • Supplier statements reconciledMonthly statements compared with your records, so differences are found early.

Who it is for

  • Businesses with more supplier bills than time to check them
  • Owners who approve payments but do not want to prepare them
  • Teams that have paid a duplicate or a fake invoice before

What is not included

Clear limits, so you always know what we do and what stays with you or another provider.

  • Sending money: you release every payment from your bank
  • Tax filings, payroll or year-end accounts, which stay with your accountant
  • Negotiating prices with suppliers
  • Holding any of your funds

How it runs

Bills are handled as they arrive, and payments go out on the days you choose.

WhenWhat happens
Every working dayNew bills captured, coded and attached in your accounting software
Before due datesBills matched; duplicates and changed bank details checked
Every weekPayment run prepared for your approval
Every monthSupplier statements reconciled with your records

What you receive

  • A weekly payment run, ready to approve and release
  • Bills due in the next 14 days
  • Anything blocked: missing approvals, mismatches, disputes
  • A monthly statement reconciliation

Response times

Agreed in writing for your time zone before work starts.

What we need from you

  • A bills inbox on your domain, for example bills@yourcompany.com
  • A limited user role in your accounting software
  • Your approval rules: who approves what, and up to what amount

Security and limits

  • You, or someone you name, release every payment
  • Bank detail changes are confirmed by phone on a number already on file, never the one in the email
  • Possible duplicates are stopped and checked before they reach a payment run

How pricing works

A fixed monthly fee based on how many bills and suppliers we process, quoted in writing. Month to month, with 30 days' notice.

Questions

Can you pay our suppliers for us?

No. We prepare the payment run and you release it from your own bank. Your money never passes through us.

Do you work with our accountant?

Yes. We keep your records tidy in the same software, so year-end work is easier for them.

What if a bill looks wrong?

We hold it, tell you why and ask the supplier for what is missing. Nothing doubtful reaches a payment run.
