Finance operations

The back office your business keeps putting off

We take on the daily finance work that slips when everyone is busy: chasing what you are owed, processing what you owe, and showing you where the cash stands every week.

Accounts receivable

Reminders, follow-ups and calls on overdue invoices, from your own accounts address. Disputes come straight back to you.

Accounts payable

Finance reporting

Who it suits

Built for businesses without a full finance team

We work best with businesses of 5 to 200 people that sell to other businesses on invoice. They usually have an accountant or bookkeeper for the year-end, but nobody whose job is to chase late payers or keep supplier bills under control day to day.

We fill that gap. We are not a replacement for your accountant: we work alongside them, in the same accounting software, and keep your records tidy for them.

  • Works in your softwareXero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent. No new system to buy.
  • Your name on everythingEmails go from your domain, signed as part of your team.
  • You stay in controlTemplates, payment runs and anything unusual are approved by you.
  • Clear weekly numbersThe same short report every Monday, whichever services you use.
